With <a href="https://caramlab.chem.ucla.edu"> Justin Caram from UCLA</a> we’re organizing a symposium at the 2027 American Physical Society
Global Physics Summit from April 11-16, 2027 in Atlanta, GA!  We’re interested in chemical design and functionality for molecular quantum information processing,
with invited talks from both theoretical and experimental perspectives and a full description found <a href="https://summit.aps.org/attend/abstracts/sorting-categories/"> 
here, under 16.01.05</a>. If this aligns with your research, abstract submission is open until October 22nd and we’re looking to add more 
excellent speakers to the symposium!
